# De-slop

Read each message against the four families below. Any hit in _fake familiarity_, or two hits anywhere else, means rewrite and check again. Facts stay; wording changes.

## Filler

Openers and closers that carry no information: pleasantries about their week, "reaching out because", "just following up", "let me know your thoughts", "looking forward to connecting".

## Buzzwords

Vocabulary buyers have learned to skip: transform, empower, streamline, revolutionize, next-level, robust, holistic, best-in-class, "at scale", "in today's fast-paced world".

## Fake familiarity

Warmth the sender hasn't earned or can't back up: praise without a specific object ("love what you're building"), invented shared interests, "I've been following your journey", claiming to have read something without saying what, first-name repetition.

## Shape

- More than one question.
- Any paragraph over three lines on mobile.
- A pitch in the invitation note, or our product name in it.
- The "I noticed / I saw" opener repeated across a batch; every draft built on the same template.
- Summarising their own profile back to them.
- Exclamation marks, emoji runs, dash-heavy rhythm.
- Asking for a call with nothing given first.

## Final check

Would the user send this, unedited, to someone they'd like to impress? Say it aloud: it should sound like a quick note between two professionals.

## Output

Each message rewritten, with a short note of what changed, e.g. `removed: buzzword ×2, second question`.
